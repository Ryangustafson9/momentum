💡 How Staff Plan Permissions Work

  • Each staff plan represents a role type (e.g., Front Desk, Trainer, Manager)
  • Select a plan from the left to view and modify its permissions
  • Toggle permissions individually or use "Select All" for entire categories
  • Click "Save Permissions" or press Ctrl+S to apply changes
  • Staff members assigned to these plans inherit the permissions automatically
